Known Limitations & Constraints
10 m SAR pixels cannot resolve footpaths or single mud-brick huts. Steep Himalayan slopes cause radar layover/shadow (filtered by slope ≤ 18°). Debris detection is a 3-signal heuristic without labeled ground truth. Missing OSM roads do not prove absence of footpaths.
